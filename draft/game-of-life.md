## Simple rules, surprising worlds.

Conway’s Game of Life is a cellular automaton devised by mathematician John Conway. Every cell is either alive or dead. At each step, all cells update together:

- A living cell survives with two or three living neighbours.
- A dead cell becomes alive with exactly three living neighbours.
- Every other cell dies or stays empty.

Click or tap the grid to toggle cells. Pause to change a pattern, use Step to advance one generation, or Randomise to start a new world. The edges of this grid are fixed: cells outside it are always dead.

This experiment revisits the Game of Life sketch from my original website, now with playback controls and room to explore.
